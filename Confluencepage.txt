Implementation/Design Update:
The data model and table relationships have been finalized for Supplier, Engagement, Contract and Business Application.
Four tables are involved:
ENGAGEMENT – Engagement details with engagement_id as the primary key.
CONTRACT – Contract details with contract_id as the primary key.
BUS_SERVICE (to be renamed to Business Application) – Business Application details with business_application_id as the primary key.
DIM_TP_ENG_CONTR_BUSAPP_REL – Relationship table providing the linkage between Supplier, Engagement, Contract and Business Application.
The relationship table contains foreign-key references to the Engagement, Contract and Business Application tables, along with supplier information. A technical identity column id has been introduced as the primary key of the relationship table, as the business/application relationship columns can contain duplicate and/or NULL values.
The relationship data has been successfully loaded into DIM_TP_ENG_CONTR_BUSAPP_REL and validated for insertion.
The source information provided for the data model is based on the available data from OneTPM, Coupa and ARIS.
