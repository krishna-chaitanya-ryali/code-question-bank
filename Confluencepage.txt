WS5 Procurement Risk Assessment Solution – High-Level Architecture
1. Solution Overview
The WS5 solution aims to automate supplier risk assessment by consolidating information from multiple HSBC source systems into a single AI-driven reporting platform. Instead of manually reviewing contracts, supplier hierarchy, business applications, operational resilience information, and incidents, the platform aggregates structured and unstructured data to generate comprehensive supplier assessment reports.
The solution leverages Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Azure AI services, FastAPI-based microservices, Azure AI Search, Cosmos DB Graph, SQL Database, and Azure OpenAI to provide contextual insights from procurement contracts and enterprise metadata.
2. Business Objective
The primary objective of the solution is to provide Procurement and Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) analysts with a unified view of supplier information by combining:
Supplier hierarchy
Engagements
Contracts
Business Applications
Business Services
Operational Resilience
Disaster Recovery
Data Recovery
Incidents
Service Dependencies
This information enables business users to make informed supplier risk and resilience decisions.

5. Procurement Contract Assistant (PCA)
PCA is the AI platform responsible for extracting information from procurement contracts.
The platform converts contract documents into structured knowledge and exposes AI-powered retrieval capabilities.
Its responsibilities include:
Contract parsing
Metadata extraction
Clause identification
Cross-reference detection
Vector indexing
Knowledge Graph creation
Context retrieval
AI-assisted question answering

8. Responsibilities of Core Services
Contract Parser
Responsible for:
Reading PDF and DOCX contracts
Converting documents into Markdown
Identifying headings
Creating structured JSON
Detecting clauses
Detecting parent-child relationships
Detecting cross references
Capturing metadata
Ingester
Responsible for:
Reading Parser JSON output
Creating embeddings
Loading documents into Azure AI Search
Creating graph nodes in Cosmos DB
Retriever
Responsible for:
Hybrid Search (Semantic + BM25)
Context Retrieval
Graph Expansion
Deep Search
Quick Search
Orchestrator
Responsible for:
Understanding user query
Coordinating microservices
Selecting retrieval strategy
Streaming responses
Returning final answer
9. Retrieval Strategy
The platform supports two retrieval modes.
Quick Search
Top-K semantic retrieval
Fast response
Limited graph expansion
Deep Search
Larger semantic search
Graph traversal
Parent-child expansion
Cross-reference expansion
Richer context for LLM
